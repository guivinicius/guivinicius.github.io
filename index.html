Things I Build

I create projects to solve my own problems, and occasionally they help others too. Some are practical; others are just for fun. Here are a few:

Pourdle — Wordle for wine enthusiasts. Guess a wine-related word each day.

Tarkov Operator — A desktop voice AI companion for Escape from Tarkov. Ask questions out loud without Alt-Tabbing.

WineTF — A retro-style sommelier simulation game. Manage a winery, serve customers, climb the leaderboard.

BCL Deals — Find the best deals at BC Liquor Stores near you.

Coffee Prices — Explore the average price per kilo of coffee in Vancouver.
